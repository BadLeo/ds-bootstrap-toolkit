# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive, and professional. Contributors are expected to communicate clearly, assume good intent, and focus on improving the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and respectful language.
- Offer technical critique without personal attacks.
- Accept feedback in good faith.
- Prioritize collaboration, clarity, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Personal attacks or hostile conduct in issues, discussions, or pull requests

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that do not meet this standard.

## Reporting

Please report conduct concerns through a private channel with repository maintainers rather than escalating in public threads.
